## 1. What the product is

Spectrum today is creator-first: someone builds a basket, stakes a reputation on it, and the
buyer must agree with every decision that creator made. The allocator flips the subject to
the **individual**: connect a wallet, say "I want AAVE, SYRUP, NVDA, BANKR", never think
about a chain — the system assembles and maintains **your own portfolio**. The front of it
is deliberately braindead-simple (the owner's five stations: home → choose → weight → confirm →
execute, no chain specifics anywhere); underneath, routing, venues and networks are computed
behind the scenes. Creators survive through **publishing**: a portfolio (or one leg of it)
can be minted as an immutable, tradable basket copy on the existing contracts — mutability
and immutability as a lifecycle, not a conflict.

**The batcher INTEGRATION CONTRACT (relayed whole via UIGuy, 2026-08-02 12:30 — the
executor consumes ALL of this; not yet deployed, addresses seat at the owner's ceremony):**
`SpectrumBatcher` per chain — `batchBuy(Leg[] legs, address fundingAsset, uint256
fundingTotal, BatchParams p) payable` · `batchRebalance(Leg[] sells, Leg[] buys, address
fundingAsset, uint256 fundingTopUp, BatchParams p) payable` · `flushBurn()` permissionless.
`Leg{venue(0=V4,1=V3,2=V2,3=BASKET), asset, ethPool(PoolKey,V4), v3Fee, v2Pair, budget,
minOut, refPriceX96, optional}` · `BatchParams{recipient, deadline, hubMinOut, aggMinBps,
feeBps}` · returns `BatchResult{spentFunding,hubOut,feeEth,ethRefunded,usdcRefunded,outs[],
skippedBitmap}` — SIMULATE it to populate the review exactly. **Seven contract-enforced
rules (never fought, only rendered):** (1) per-leg `minOut` AND `hubMinOut` non-zero
whenever funding ≠ leg denomination, zero floors REVERT; (2) funding = native ETH or the
chain's settlement asset ONLY; (3) `recipient` explicit, never assumed `msg.sender`;
(4) `optional=false` legs revert the whole batch, `optional=true` skip + refund wei-exact —
**that flag IS the consent surface, rendered at sign time** (the review owns marking thin
legs optional); (5) refunds may arrive in a DIFFERENT denomination than funding (skipped
BASKET leg under ETH funding refunds settlement) — shown, never silently netted;
(6) a BASKET leg ≈ 6 plain legs toward the cap (runs the basket's inner acquisition);
(7) `feeBps` is calldata — 50 per the final ruling, bytecode-capped 200, and it BURNS
PRISM (copy says so; not revenue). Evidence:
`spectrum-contracts/docs/BATCHER-AUDIT-ROUND-1-2026-08-02.md`.

**Batcher facts for the planner (contracts, measured on fork rehearsals, 2026-08-02):**
one funding asset in → N swaps in ONE PoolManager unlock → outputs to an explicit recipient,
no custody at rest; 12 plain legs ≈ 726k gas (≈2.4% of an ETH block; hard cap 32 legs, a
BASKET leg ≈ 6 plain); `batchRebalance(sells, buys, …)` settles sells from the payer and
funds buys from proceeds — one tx per chain per diff; **partial fills are consent-shaped**:
per-leg optional flag, required-by-default, skipped legs refund wei-exact in the same tx —
**the review screen owns marking thin legs optional**. Ceremony-ready; only the owner's Ledger
signing remains.

**Routing policy — HARDENED by the PM's seam review (UIGuy, 2026-08-01, desk item 25; he
wrote the LiFi seam). Six requirements, the first two BLOCKING before Phase 3 builds:**
1. **Net-out means net of GAS**: `LifiQuote` today drops LiFi's `estimate.gasCosts` —
   extend the seam to surface it, price the direct route's gas the same way (`gas.ts`),
   and compare AFTER gas; a gross comparison systematically favors multi-hop aggregator
   routes on Ethereum and can invert the winner on small legs.
2. **Their floor is a claim, ours is a check**: LiFi's `toAmountMin` is read from their
   API and never verified inside their calldata — read the SIMULATED output (recipient
   balance delta in the eth_call we already run) and refuse to sign below OUR floor.
3. **Basket tokens are excluded from the race by construction** — a basket mints at NAV
   through its own router; an aggregator finding a thin third-party pool must never be
   labeled "best route".
4. **The integrator fee sits INSIDE the compared quote**, never added after — and DECIDED
   2026-08-01: never the batcher fee AND LiFi's integrator fee on the same route (XOR);
   check the stack against the kit's frontend fee tag (the seam sends no fee params today;
   this is a seam change).
5. **Never block on the aggregator**: bounded concurrency + hard timeout at N legs
   (12 legs = 12 calls at review + 12 at re-quote); on slow/429 the direct route wins
   uncontested.
6. **Approve only the winner, exact-amount, AFTER the execution re-quote** — the winner
   can flip between review and execution, and a review-time approval leaves a standing
   allowance to a third-party spender.

**Routing policy (the owner, 2026-08-01: "ensure… an aggregator that routes more efficiently
than just Uniswap… best pricing on each chain"):** never single-venue-by-default. Per buy,
quote the **aggregator seam** (LiFi — already vendored in this kit and proven on same-chain
legs in TradePrism; it aggregates the DEX aggregators) **against the kit's own best-pool
direct route**, take the better *executable* net-out, simulate the exact bytes, floors
enforced — the winner is re-quoted at execution, never cached. Two honest caveats are part
of the policy, not exceptions to it: **(a) aggregator coverage is per-asset TRANSIENT**
(the PRISM lesson: routed at 16:00, "no available quotes" at 20:30 the same day) so the
direct-pool fallback stays permanently warm; **(b) Robinhood Chain (4663) likely has no
external aggregator coverage** — there the kit's own multi-venue pool detection IS the
aggregator, and the UI never pretends otherwise. Fee-lane note for G1/G2: LiFi carries an
**integrator-fee parameter** — a possible 0.5% capture on aggregated routes with NO custom
periphery contract; flagged to the fee decision, not decided here.
